# Code of Conduct

Illam follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Welcome people of every background and level of experience.
- Criticise ideas, not people.
- Harassment, personal attacks and sharing others' private information are not tolerated.
  That includes anyone's financial data, which this project handles.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/kriznaraj/illam/security/advisories/new), or by
opening a minimal issue that asks for contact. Reports are handled confidentially, and the
maintainer may remove content or ban participants who break these rules.
